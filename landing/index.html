Simple on the outside,
smart underneath.

Pick a cursor style, set the zoom, then drag a box around what you want to record. That's the whole workflow.

Your cursor, impossible to miss

A soft yellow halo follows the mouse and every click sends out a ripple, live on screen and saved in the video.

  • Halo and ripples, halo only, ripples only, or off
  • Left clicks in yellow, right clicks in blue

Auto-zoom that follows your mouse

When you stop, the video zooms in where you clicked, pans with your cursor, and eases back out when you're done.

  • Choose how close (1.2× to 3×) and how smooth
  • Compare before and after with a drag

Your face, wherever you want it

Turn on the camera and your webcam floats in a bubble on top of everything. Drag it anywhere or snap it to a corner. It's recorded with your screen.

  • Small, medium or large; circle or rounded
  • Mirrored like a selfie

Export the way you need it

When you press Stop, choose the resolution, quality and sound, name the file, and save. MP4 plays everywhere.

  • Original, 1080p or 720p
  • Saved only to the folder you choose
