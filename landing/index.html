What viewers see: the halo follows your mouse, clicks ripple, and the video zooms in where you click.

Made for tutorials, demos and walkthroughs.

The three things that make a screen recording easy to follow are built in, and you can switch each one off.

Your cursor, easy to spot

A yellow halo follows the mouse and every click sends out a ripple. You see it while you record, and it's saved in the video.

Auto-zoom that follows the mouse

When you stop, the video zooms in where you clicked, pans with your cursor, and eases back out. Set how close and how smooth.

Your face in a bubble

Turn on the camera and your webcam floats in a circle you can drag anywhere or snap to a corner. It's recorded with your screen.

The app on Windows. Pick a cursor style, set the zoom, then drag a box around what you want to record.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There are no paid features, watermarks or time limits.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that normal?

Yes. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows shows that warning for new apps. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Does anything get uploaded?

No. Recording, auto-zoom and saving all happen on your PC. The only thing we receive is the name and email you enter here.

What do I need?

Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) or Windows 11. A webcam and microphone are optional.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. There is a Linux build for people comfortable compiling from source.
