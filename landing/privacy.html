Screen Recorder

Privacy

Applies to the Screen Recorder app (Windows, Mac, Linux, Android), the Screen Recorder browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave), and this website. Last updated 3 November 2026.

Your recordings never leave your device. Recording, editing, auto-zoom and exporting all happen on your computer, phone or in your browser. We never receive your videos, your screen, your camera or your microphone.

What we collect

  • When you download from this website: the name and email you enter, which system you downloaded for, your country (from your internet address) and your browser type. We use it to count downloads and, only if you tick the box, to email you about new versions.
  • When you buy Pro: your name, email and the plan you chose, to create your licence key and send your receipt. Payments are handled by Bachs; we never see your card or bank details.
  • Licence checks: the app and the extension send your licence key to this website now and then to confirm Pro is active. Nothing else is sent with it.
  • Website visits: visit statistics from our analytics service (Sabilytics), so we know which pages are useful.

The browser extension

The extension asks Chrome for these permissions, and uses them only for recording:

  • Tab capture and offscreen document: to record the tab, window or screen you choose, only after you press Start.
  • Microphone and camera (only if you turn them on): to record your voice and show your face in a bubble.
  • Read and change data on websites: to draw the cursor halo, click ripples, camera bubble and the Stop button on the page you're recording. It doesn't read or collect what's on the page.
  • Storage: to keep your recordings and settings in your browser until you delete them.
  • Downloads: to save the videos you export to your Downloads folder.

The extension doesn't track your browsing, doesn't show ads, and doesn't sell or share data with anyone.

How long we keep it

Download and purchase records are kept while your licence is active and as long as tax rules require. Ask us to delete your download sign-up at any time.

Your choices

  • Delete recordings any time from Recordings in the app or the extension.
  • Ask us any time to stop sending you update emails.
  • To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, reply to any email from us or open an issue on GitHub.
